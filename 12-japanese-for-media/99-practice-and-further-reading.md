# Practice and Further Reading

The practice below trains the three decoding skills of this chapter: hearing who is speaking in anime dialogue, reading the sound effects and contractions of manga, and unpacking news headlines. Work through it in order, and say the Japanese aloud as you go; the answers are at the back of the book.

## Practice

### 12.1 Japanese Through Anime

1. Give the meaning of each anime line: まさか！, そんな…！, 俺に任せろ！, やれやれ.
2. Give the reading and meaning: 仲間, 異世界, 必殺技, 部活, 告白.
3. Which character type is speaking? (a) 「わしの若いころはのう…」 (b) 「お帰りなさいませ、ご主人様」 (c) 「べ、別にあんたのためじゃないんだからね！」 (d) 「わたくし、紅茶しか飲みませんの。よろしくってよ」
4. Rewrite each anime line as something you could say to a friend: 信じろ！, 俺に任せろ！, 行くぞ！
5. A villain has just been told his plan failed and shouts 「失敗しただと！？」. What is he expressing?
6. Your coworker loves anime. Which of these would be risky to use when talking to her at work, and why: 俺, お前, 〜ぞ, 任せて?

### 12.2 Reading Manga

7. A manga page has four panels in a two-by-two grid. In what order do you read them?
8. Match each sound effect to what it describes: ドカン, ザーザー, ガシャン, ポタポタ. Choices: dripping, glass breaking, an explosion, pouring rain.
9. Match each mimetic word to a feeling or state: ドキドキ, モジモジ, ニヤリ, ガーン. Choices: a sly grin, shock, a pounding heart, fidgeting shyly.
10. Translate: 教室がシーンとなった。
11. Write the full (uncontracted) form of each: 食べてる, 忘れちゃった, 行かなきゃ, 知らないんだ.
12. A speech bubble shows the kanji 本気 with the furigana マジ. What does the reader take from the kanji, and what from the furigana?
13. Translate: 宿題、まだやってない。やらなきゃ。

### 12.3 Reading the News

14. Which countries do these headline abbreviations stand for: 米, 中, 韓, 露, 英, 仏, 独, 豪?
15. Give the reading and meaning: 首相, 与党, 野党, 選挙, 地震, 避難指示.
16. Convert: 令和8年 and 平成7年 to Western years. Which months does 2026年度 cover?
17. Read and give the value: 1億2000万人, 約3000人, 100人余り, 3割強.
18. Unpack the headline into a full sentence and translate it: 日米首脳会談、来月開催へ
19. Unpack and translate: 円安受け　物価上昇続く
20. A man named Yamada is arrested. How will the news refer to him, and how after he is indicted? Why don't reporters use his bare name?
21. Put these JMA warnings in order from least to most serious: 警報, 特別警報, 注意報, 危険警報.

## Further Reading

- [NHK News Web Easy](https://news.web.nhk/news/easy/) — NHK's own simplified news for learners, with switchable furigana, pop-up definitions and audio; read one article a day.
- [NHK World: Learn Japanese](https://www3.nhk.or.jp/nhkworld/en/learnjapanese/) — free courses and audio from Japan's public broadcaster, with slow, clear speech for building listening stamina.
- [Jisho.org](https://jisho.org) — a free online dictionary with kanji lookup by radical and handwriting, ideal for the words in manga and headlines.
- [Japanese Onomatopoeia (Tofugu)](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese/japanese-onomatopoeia/) — a long, readable guide to giongo and gitaigo, with the sound patterns that make new ones guessable.
- [Japanese with Anime](https://www.japanesewithanime.com) — explanations of grammar, slang and role language illustrated with real anime and manga examples.
- [少年ジャンプ+ (Shōnen Jump+)](https://shonenjumpplus.com) — Shueisha's official manga service, with many first chapters free in the original Japanese.

Anime, manga and the news are mostly about understanding other people's Japanese.
