# Chapter 8 — Arts, Entertainment and Sport

A Japanese person might spend a Saturday morning at an ikebana class, the afternoon shouting fight songs in the outfield stands at a ballpark, and the evening watching a Vtuber stream while 草 and 888888 scroll past in the chat. None of them would find that combination strange. Japan keeps its oldest arts alive alongside some of the most inventive pop culture on the planet, and the two borrow from each other constantly: a city-pop song samples a koto, a battle anime quotes a noh play, a sumo commentator talks about 残心.

This chapter is a tour of what Japan does for fun, for beauty and for glory, from the tea room to the sumo ring. It is also a vocabulary chapter in disguise. Every art, genre and sport has its own words, and many of them have escaped into everyday speech: 推し, ドキドキ, 逆転, 一期一会. Know them and you can follow a conversation about a drama, a match or a concert, read the comments under a music video and, perhaps best of all, share the things you love with Japanese friends in their own language.

Expect to recognize a lot. Many learners come to Japanese through anime, games or music, and this chapter is where that passion pays off in vocabulary.

## In This Chapter

- Name the core vocabulary of the tea ceremony, ikebana, kabuki, noh and calligraphy, and explain aesthetic ideas such as wabi-sabi, ma and yūgen
- Recognize the main instruments and genres of traditional music, from gagaku and koto to shamisen, enka and folk song
- Trace Japanese popular music from kayōkyoku and city pop through the million-selling 1990s to the Vocaloid generation and today's streaming stars
- Talk about anime, manga, games, idols and kawaii culture with the words fans actually use
- Read live chat and social-media posts, including the Vtuber fan vocabulary and Niconico-era memes
- Follow a baseball broadcast and a sumo tournament, from 逆転サヨナラ to 寄り切り and the banzuke

## Before You Start

You should be comfortable reading kana and know a few hundred kanji ([chapter 1](../01-writing-system/README.md)), and you will get more from the chat and fan language if you have read about casual speech in [chapter 3](../03-politeness/README.md) and internet slang in [chapter 5](../05-living-language/README.md). [Section 8.1](01-traditional-arts.md) builds on the cultural background of [chapter 7](../07-customs-and-daily-life/README.md), but each section stands on its own.
