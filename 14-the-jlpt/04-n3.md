# N3: The Great Leap

Below N3, Japanese is a set of foundations: kana, basic kanji, the core grammar. Above N3, you are building fluency. N3 is the bridge between them, and it is a long one. Many learners find it the hardest single step of the whole journey, not because N3 is harder than N2 or N1, but because it is where structured textbook learning ends and the open sea of real Japanese begins.

N3 was added when the JLPT was revised in 2010, precisely to fill the large gap between the old beginner and upper-intermediate levels. Its official description is "the ability to understand Japanese used in everyday situations to a certain degree": reading everyday written materials and grasping the main points of somewhat difficult texts such as newspaper headlines, and following conversations at near-natural speed. This is the level where Japanese starts to feel like a usable skill.

## What N3 Covers

| Area | Target (est.) |
|---|---|
| Vocabulary | ~3,750 words |
| Kanji | ~650 characters |
| Grammar | ~250 patterns in total |
| Reading | Articles, letters, explanations and short opinion pieces |
| Listening | Near-natural conversations on a range of everyday topics |

Going from about 1,500 words at N4 to about 3,750 at N3 more than doubles your vocabulary, and the new words are increasingly abstract. This is where systematic vocabulary study stops being optional.

## Key Grammar at N3

### Reason and Cause

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 〜ために | In order to; because of | 試験の**ために**勉強する。 |
| 〜せいで | Because of (a bad result) | 雨の**せいで**遅れた。 |
| 〜おかげで | Thanks to (a good result) | 君の**おかげで**合格した。 |
| 〜によって | Depending on; by means of | 人**によって**違う。 |
| 〜わけだ | No wonder; that explains it | 疲れている**わけだ**。 |
| 〜からといって | Just because ~ (doesn't mean) | 金持ちだ**からといって**幸せとは限らない。 |

### Change

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 〜ていく | Change continuing from now on | 変わっ**ていく**。 |
| 〜てくる | Change up to now; something emerging | 分かっ**てきた**。 |
| 〜ようになる | Come to be able to | 泳げる**ようになった**。 |
| 〜なくなる | Stop being, no longer | 痛く**なくなった**。 |
| 〜に変わる | Change into ~ | 雨が雪**に変わる**。 |

### Scope and Limits

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 〜だけでなく〜も | Not only ~ but also | 英語**だけでなく**日本語**も**話せる。 |
| 〜ばかり | Nothing but, only ever | 遊んで**ばかり**いる。 |
| 〜さえ〜ば | If only ~ | 時間**さえ**あれ**ば**行ける。 |
| 〜くらい / ぐらい | About; to the extent that | 3日**くらい**かかる。 |
| 〜について | About, concerning | 日本語**について**話す。 |

### Certainty and Appearance

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 〜はずがない | Can't possibly be | そんな**はずがない**。 |
| 〜にちがいない | Must surely be | 彼**にちがいない**。 |
| 〜らしい | Apparently (hearsay) | 雨が降る**らしい**。 |
| 〜ようだ | Seems (based on what I observe) | 疲れている**ようだ**。 |
| 〜みたいだ | Seems, looks like (casual ようだ) | 知っている**みたいだ**。 |
| 〜かもしれない | Might, perhaps | 来る**かもしれない**。 |
| 〜と思われる | It is thought that (formal) | これが原因**と思われる**。 |

### More て-Form Patterns

| Pattern | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 〜てはじめて | Only after doing ~ (do you realize or can you) |
| 〜てしょうがない | Can't help feeling ~, extremely |
| 〜てたまらない | Unbearably ~ |
| 〜てならない | Can't help feeling ~ (more formal) |
| 〜ていられない | Can't afford to keep doing ~ |

> **Warning:** らしい, ようだ, みたいだ and そうだ all translate as "seems", and N3 tests the differences. らしい reports what you heard, ようだ and みたいだ infer from what you observe, and the stem + そうだ of N4 describes how something looks right now.

## Vocabulary Strategy for N3

N3 means learning around 2,000 new words. Four habits make that manageable:

1. **Daily reviews.** About 20 new Anki cards a day, with at least 20 minutes of review.
2. **Words in context.** Vocabulary met in real reading sticks far better than vocabulary met in a list.
3. **Word families.** Learn 悲しい (sad), 悲しむ (to grieve) and 悲しみ (sorrow) together.
4. **Suffixes.** Recognize productive endings: 〜的 (-ic, -al, as in 経済的, economic), 〜化 (-ization, as in 国際化, internationalization) and 〜者 (person, as in 参加者, participant).

These domains supply many of the new words:

**Abstract ideas:** 理解 (understanding), 判断 (judgment), 影響 (influence), 状況 (situation), 原因 (cause), 結果 (result), 可能性 (possibility), 必要性 (necessity), 効果 (effect).

**Emotions:** 感動 (being deeply moved), 不安 (anxiety), 安心 (relief), 失望 (disappointment), 期待 (expectation, hope), 後悔 (regret), 満足 (satisfaction), 我慢 (putting up with something).

**Society:** 社会 (society), 文化 (culture), 伝統 (tradition), 習慣 (custom, habit), 礼儀 (manners), 差別 (discrimination), 平等 (equality), 協力 (cooperation), 競争 (competition).

## Reading at N3

N3 reading is a clear step up from N4. Passages are longer, often several paragraphs with an argument or a story; some questions ask you to infer what is not stated directly; and others ask what the writer thinks or wants. Topics include health habits, travel, changes in technology, customs and advice columns.

A reliable approach:

1. Read the question first, so you know what to look for.
2. Skim the passage for its main idea.
3. Read the relevant part carefully.
4. For inference questions, watch for signals such as 〜といえる (it can be said), 〜と考えられる (it is thought) and 〜ようだ (it seems).

## Listening at N3

N3 listening uses conversations at close to natural speed: two people making plans or decisions, announcements and instructions, short stories, and conversations where you must work out how someone feels or what they intend. Note the setting first (where are they, and who are they to each other?), listen for the main point rather than every word, and train with NHK News Web Easy audio, official practice tests and anime.

## A Twelve-Week Plan

| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Vocabulary ([Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net) and [Nihongo Sō-matome N3](https://www.ask-books.com/somatome/)) |
| 4–5 | Grammar ([Shin Kanzen Master N3 grammar](https://www.3anet.co.jp/np/en/)) |
| 6–7 | Kanji (about 20 a day) |
| 8 | Reading ([NHK News Web Easy](https://www3.nhk.or.jp/news/easy/), then regular NHK articles) |
| 9 | Listening (official practice audio, anime, NHK) |
| 10 | First full practice test and analysis of weak points |
| 11 | Targeted review of weak sections |
| 12 | Second full practice test and final review |

## What N3 Lets You Do

With N3 you can talk about most everyday topics, read NHK News Web Easy comfortably, follow most of a slice-of-life anime, read manga for older teens (some without furigana), write clear emails and messages, partly follow a news broadcast with effort and travel independently in Japan, reading signs and asking for help.

Many learners use *Tobira* as their main textbook at this stage and remember it as a turning point: the first book that feels like real Japanese rather than textbook Japanese. That feeling is the bridge in action.

## Summary

- N3 sits between beginner and upper-intermediate and was added in the 2010 revision of the JLPT to bridge that gap.
- Vocabulary more than doubles from N4 to N3 (about 1,500 to about 3,750 words, as estimates), so steady daily vocabulary study is essential.
- N3 grammar expresses cause, change, limits and degrees of certainty, and らしい, ようだ, みたいだ and そうだ must be told apart.
- Reading passages run to several paragraphs and include inference questions about the writer's opinion.
- N3 needs an overall score of 95 out of 180, higher than N2's 90, with at least 19 in each section.
