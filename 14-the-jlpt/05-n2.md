# N2: Near Fluent

Open a Japanese newspaper at N3 and you can pick out the topic of each article. Open it at N2 and you can follow the argument, notice where the writer concedes a point and spot the sentence where they quietly disagree. N2 is where Japanese becomes useful in the real world: it is a common requirement in job ads for non-Japanese staff, a benchmark for university admission in Japan and the level at which you can follow the news.

Officially, N2 is "the ability to understand Japanese used in everyday situations, and in a variety of circumstances to a certain degree". In practice that means reading newspaper and magazine articles, commentaries and clearly written explanations on general topics, and following conversations and news at nearly natural speed. It is a genuinely significant milestone, and passing it changes how employers and schools in Japan see you.

## What N2 Covers

| Area | Target (est.) |
|---|---|
| Vocabulary | ~6,000 words |
| Kanji | ~1,000 characters |
| Grammar | Many formal and written patterns on top of N3 |
| Reading | Newspaper articles, commentaries, formal letters, longer explanations |
| Listening | Nearly natural speed: news, discussions, longer talks |

The jump from about 3,750 words at N3 to about 6,000 is another 2,000-plus words, many of them abstract or formal, with a large share of 漢語 (*kango*, Sino-Japanese compounds) such as 傾向 and 前提.

## N2 Grammar: Key Patterns

### Formal and Written Expressions

These patterns are the connective tissue of newspapers, reports and official notices.

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 〜において / 〜における | In, at (formal) | 会議**において**決定された。 |
| 〜に関して / 〜に関する | Regarding, concerning | この問題**に関して**意見がある。 |
| 〜に対して / 〜に対する | Toward, against, in contrast to | 批判**に対して**反論した。 |
| 〜を通じて / 〜を通して | Through, via | 交渉**を通じて**解決した。 |
| 〜として | As, in the role of | 代表**として**参加する。 |
| 〜にわたって | Over, throughout (a period or area) | 3年**にわたって**研究した。 |
| 〜をはじめ(として) | Starting with, including | 東京**をはじめ**、各地で雪が降った。 |
| 〜に伴って | As, along with (a change) | 高齢化**に伴って**問題が増える。 |
| 〜に応じて | In response to, according to | 予算**に応じて**選ぶ。 |

### Degree and Emphasis

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 〜にすぎない | No more than, merely | 噂**にすぎない**。 |
| 〜にほかならない | Is nothing other than | これは努力の結果**にほかならない**。 |
| 〜こそ | Precisely (emphasis) | 今**こそ**チャンスだ。 |
| 〜さえ | Even | 子供で**さえ**知っている。 |
| 〜ほど〜ない | Not as ~ as | 思った**ほど**難しく**ない**。 |
| 〜に至っては | As for (the most extreme case) | 彼**に至っては**全く知らない。 |

### Stance and Opinion

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 〜に違いない | Must be (strong conviction) | 彼が犯人**に違いない**。 |
| 〜わけがない | There's no way that ~ | できる**わけがない**。 |
| 〜わけにはいかない | Can't (for social or moral reasons) | 諦める**わけにはいかない**。 |
| 〜ものだ | Used to (nostalgia); that's how it is | 昔はよく遊んだ**ものだ**。 |
| 〜ものではない | One shouldn't | 嘘をつく**ものではない**。 |

### Concession and Contrast

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 〜ものの | Although | 行った**ものの**、何もできなかった。 |
| 〜にもかかわらず | Despite | 反対**にもかかわらず**実行した。 |
| 〜とはいえ | Even so, that said | 忙しい**とはいえ**、連絡はできる。 |
| 〜ながら(も) | While, even though | 知り**ながら**黙っていた。 |
| 〜からには | Now that, since | 決めた**からには**頑張る。 |

> **Tip:** Concession patterns are the writer's way of saying "yes, but". In N2 reading, the sentence after 〜ものの or 〜とはいえ usually carries the writer's real point, so it is often where the answer is.

## N2 Vocabulary: Key Domains

**News and current affairs:** 報道 (reporting), 政策 (policy), 経済 (economy), 予算 (budget), 外交 (diplomacy), 条約 (treaty), 制裁 (sanctions), 議会 (parliament, assembly), 改革 (reform), 批判 (criticism), 支持 (support), 反対 (opposition).

**Academic and abstract:** 論理 (logic), 証明 (proof), 仮説 (hypothesis), 根拠 (grounds, basis), 矛盾 (contradiction), 概念 (concept), 傾向 (tendency), 観点 (point of view), 前提 (premise), 結論 (conclusion), 比較 (comparison), 検討 (examination, consideration).

**Formal connectives:** 以上 (the above; that is all), 以下 (the following), 一方 (on the other hand), なお (in addition, note that), ただし (however, provided that), これに対し (in contrast), したがって (therefore), よって (thus).

**Formal and literary patterns:** 〜べき (should, ought to), 〜べからず (must not; seen on old signs), 〜たる (as one who is ~), 〜しかるべき (appropriate, proper), 〜にして (while being; only by). Most of these become active N1 grammar; at N2 you mainly need to recognize them.

## N2 Kanji: About 350 More

N2 takes you to roughly 1,000 kanji, adding about 350 to the N3 base. They open up the vocabulary of news, science and formal writing:

| Theme | Kanji |
|---|---|
| Law and politics | 省 裁 判 罪 刑 議 庁 権 |
| Science and technology | 科 技 術 研 究 験 証 測 機 器 |
| Economics | 経 済 率 株 投 資 負 債 益 |
| Formal writing | 論 述 析 察 観 念 義 則 規 制 |

## Reading at N2

N2 reading tests whether you can understand newspaper and magazine articles, follow editorials and arguments, read formal notices and letters, handle longer explanatory texts and identify implicit meaning and the writer's intent. Time is the main enemy.

Four habits help:

1. Build speed with daily reading; N2 passages are longer and the clock is tight.
2. Map each paragraph's structure: claim, support, conclusion.
3. Study how writers signal concession and contrast (〜ものの, 〜とはいえ, 一方).
4. Read real editorials, such as the 社説 (*shasetsu*, editorial) columns of the major national newspapers.

## Listening at N2

N2 listening includes news-style announcements at close to broadcast speed, conversations between several speakers, lectures and longer monologues, and dialogues where you must infer someone's attitude. The key skill is inference: working out what a speaker actually thinks when politeness keeps them from saying it directly.

## A Sixteen-Week Plan

| Phase | Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Vocabulary | 1–4 | 2,000-plus new words ([Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net) and [Nihongo Sō-matome N2](https://www.ask-books.com/somatome/)) |
| Grammar | 5–8 | Every pattern in [Shin Kanzen Master N2 grammar](https://www.3anet.co.jp/np/en/) |
| Kanji | 9–10 | About 350 new kanji and their compounds |
| Reading | 11–12 | Newspaper articles, [NHK News](https://www3.nhk.or.jp/news/) and practice passages |
| Listening | 13–14 | [NHK radio](https://www.nhk.or.jp/radio/), official practice audio, drama without subtitles |
| Practice tests | 15–16 | Three or more full practice tests, with targeted review |

Plan on two hours or more a day. N2 rewards treating your study like a course, not a hobby you fit in when you can.

## What N2 Opens

**Career and study.** Many Japanese companies list N2 as a requirement for non-Japanese applicants, universities in Japan use N2 and above as a benchmark for admission, and N2 earns 10 points in Japan's points-based immigration system for highly skilled professionals.

**Everyday ability.** You can read most newspaper articles with a dictionary, follow much of a news broadcast, read most manga without furigana, take part in most workplace conversations and write professional emails and reports (the conventions are in [chapter 13](../13-business-japanese/README.md)).

**Immersion.** At N2 you can enjoy most Japanese media without subtitles and understand enough to keep learning from it. This is where immersion starts to accelerate your progress.

## Summary

- N2 means understanding Japanese in everyday situations and, to a degree, in a variety of circumstances, including newspaper articles and near-natural-speed speech.
- Vocabulary grows to about 6,000 words and kanji to about 1,000 (community estimates), with many formal Sino-Japanese compounds.
- N2 grammar is dominated by formal written patterns (〜において, 〜に関して, 〜に伴って) and concession patterns (〜ものの, 〜とはいえ).
- In N2 reading, the sentence after a concession usually carries the writer's real point.
- N2 is a common hiring requirement for non-Japanese staff and earns 10 points in Japan's immigration points system for highly skilled professionals.
