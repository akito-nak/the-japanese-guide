# Practice and Further Reading

Work through the items in order: they start with single particles and words and build up to whole sentences and real situations. Write your answers down, say the Japanese out loud, and only then check them against the answers at the back of this book. Where several answers are possible, the back gives a model answer; yours may differ and still be right.

## Practice

### 2.1 Grammar Basics

1. Fill in each blank with は, が, を, に or で.
   1. 私＿学生です。 (I am a student.)
   2. 毎朝コーヒー＿飲みます。 (I drink coffee every morning.)
   3. 来週、京都＿行きます。 (I'm going to Kyoto next week.)
   4. 図書館＿本を読みます。 (I read books at the library.)
   5. 誰＿来ましたか。 (Who came?)
2. Fill in each blank with は, が, を, に or で. Some sentences need two particles.
   1. 公園＿犬＿います。 (There is a dog in the park.)
   2. バス＿学校に行きます。 (I go to school by bus.)
   3. 7時＿起きます。 (I get up at seven.)
   4. 私＿猫＿好きです。 (I like cats.)
   5. レストラン＿寿司＿食べました。 (I ate sushi at a restaurant.)
3. Put the words in the correct Japanese order to make a sentence.
   1. 食べます / 私は / りんごを
   2. 行きます / 東京に / 田中さんは / 明日
   3. 図書館で / 読みます / 本を / 妹は
4. Translate into Japanese, using polite (ます) forms.
   1. I drink tea.
   2. I go to school by train.
   3. My friend watches TV at home.
5. Sort these verbs into godan (u-verbs) and ichidan (ru-verbs): 帰る, 寝る, 走る, 起きる, 知る, 見る, 入る, 食べる.
6. Give the polite ます form of each verb: 書く, 飲む, 食べる, 帰る, する, 来る.
7. Give the casual negative ない form of each verb: 話す, 買う, 見る, 待つ, する, 来る.
8. Give the te-form of each verb: 書く, 泳ぐ, 待つ, 読む, 遊ぶ, 話す, 行く, 食べる.
9. Conjugate each adjective into the present negative, the past and the past negative (casual forms): 高い, 静か, いい.
10. Each sentence contains one mistake. Correct it.
    1. 昨日は美しいでした。
    2. 私は弟があります。
    3. 誰は来ましたか。
    4. 大きいの犬がいます。
11. Which counter would you use for each? (a) sheets of paper, (b) cars, (c) books, (d) cups of coffee, (e) people, (f) pens.
12. Translate into Japanese: (a) Where is the station? (b) What is this? (c) When are you going? (polite)

### 2.2 Core Vocabulary

13. Read each number aloud and write it in digits: 三万, 千五百, 二十四, 百万.
14. Give the reading and English meaning: 水曜日, 金曜日, 日曜日, 四月, 九月.
15. Translate into English: 駅, 病院, 切符, 終電, 野菜, 忙しい.
16. Translate into Japanese: tomorrow, last week, younger sister, to borrow, cheap, convenience store.
17. Give the opposite of each word: 大きい, 新しい, 高い (expensive), 多い, 開ける, 乗る.
18. Choose the right word: (a) Today is very (暑い / 熱い). (b) This soup is (暑い / 熱い). (c) Winter in Hokkaido is (寒い / 冷たい). (d) I'd like a (寒い / 冷たい) drink.
19. Choose the right family word. (a) Telling a coworker about your own mother. (b) Asking a friend about their mother. (c) Telling a teacher that your older brother lives in Osaka.
20. Translate into Japanese: (a) How much is it? (b) I don't understand. (c) Please speak slowly. (d) Excuse me, where is the bank?

### 2.3 Phrases and Idioms

21. What do you say in each situation? (a) Right before you start eating. (b) To the restaurant staff on your way out. (c) Stepping into a friend's apartment. (d) Leaving home in the morning. (e) Arriving home in the evening.
22. Your family member says いってきます as they leave. How do you reply? And when they come home and say ただいま?
23. You're leaving the office before your manager. What do you say as you go, and what will your manager most likely say back? Why should you avoid ご苦労様です here?
24. At a restaurant, how do you ask (a) for the check, (b) for separate checks and (c) what the staff recommend?
25. A convenience store clerk asks 袋は大丈夫ですか. Give two ways to say you don't need a bag.
26. You invite a classmate to a party on Saturday and they answer 土曜日はちょっと… What do they mean, and what is the best thing to do next?
27. Match each idiom to its meaning: (a) 猫に小判, (b) 一石二鳥, (c) 花より団子, (d) 七転び八起き, (e) 井の中の蛙. Meanings: (1) never give up; (2) someone with a narrow view of the world; (3) wasting something valuable on someone who can't appreciate it; (4) substance over style; (5) two goals achieved with one action.
28. Choose the best sound word: (a) The rain is pouring down: 雨が＿＿降っている. (b) I'm so irritated: ＿＿する. (c) This futon is soft and fluffy: ＿＿だ. (d) She speaks English fluently: 英語が＿＿だ. Choose from ペラペラ, ザーザー, フワフワ, イライラ.
29. Name three aizuchi you could use while a Japanese friend tells you a story, and explain why using them matters.

## Further Reading

- [Tae Kim's Guide to Learning Japanese](https://guidetojapanese.org/learn/grammar) — a free, complete grammar course that explains particles and conjugation from the Japanese point of view rather than through English.
- [Jisho](https://jisho.org/) — a fast, free Japanese–English dictionary that shows readings, example sentences and the verb group of every verb.
- [Marugoto: Japanese Language and Culture](https://www.marugoto.org/en/) — the Japan Foundation's official course, built around real tasks and set phrases for everyday situations.
- [Erin's Challenge! I Can Speak Japanese](https://www.erin.jpf.go.jp/en/) — free Japan Foundation video episodes in which a high school student's daily life shows grammar and set phrases in natural context.
- [NHK World Easy Japanese](https://www.nhk.or.jp/lesson/en/) — the public broadcaster's free beginner course, with short audio episodes that drill exactly the phrases and patterns in this chapter.
- [Genki: An Integrated Course in Elementary Japanese](https://genki3.japantimes.co.jp/en/) — the widely used university textbook by Eri Banno and colleagues (The Japan Times), with a free companion site of audio and practice.
- [Kotobank](https://kotobank.jp/) — a free Japanese-language dictionary site; look up any idiom, yojijukugo or proverb from [section 2.3](03-phrases-and-idioms.md) to read its origin and usage once you can read some Japanese.

You can now build a sentence, conjugate a verb and say the right thing at the table and the office door. The next question shapes every Japanese sentence you will ever say: who are you talking to? [Chapter 3](../03-politeness/README.md) maps the levels of politeness, from casual speech with friends to the honorific language of business and service.
