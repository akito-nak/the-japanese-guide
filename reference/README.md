# Reference

The pages in this part are for looking things up, not for reading straight through. Keep them open while you work through the practice, and come back whenever a kana, a conjugation or a particle slips your mind, or when you need a new tool or the source behind a claim.

- **Kana Charts**: every hiragana and katakana, with voiced sounds, combinations and the extended katakana used for foreign words.
- **Grammar and Conjugation Tables**: verb groups and their forms, adjectives, the copula, the essential particles and the politeness levels side by side.
- **Resources for Learning Japanese**: dictionaries, courses, graded readers, listening, pitch-accent tools, tutors, exams, and the research on learning that chapter 1 draws on.
