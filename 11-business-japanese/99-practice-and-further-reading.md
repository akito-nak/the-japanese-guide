# Practice and Further Reading

Business Japanese is learned by saying it, so read every Japanese answer aloud, then say it again faster, as if a client were waiting. Where an item asks you to write, write the full sentence by hand before checking it. Model answers are at the back of this book; any answer with the same politeness level and the same set phrase counts as right.

## Practice

### 11.1 Workplace Language and Introductions

1. Read these aloud and give their meanings: 上司, 同僚, 取引先, 担当者.
2. You are writing a cover letter to a company. Which word do you use for "your company": 御社, 貴社 or 弊社?
3. Translate into English: 弊社の担当者からご連絡いたします。
4. Translate into Japanese: "My name is Smith, and I'll be joining you from today." Use the humble verb for your name.
5. A client hands you their business card. What do you say as you receive it, and where do you put the card during the meeting?
6. Match each term to its meaning: 正社員, 派遣社員, 残業, 有給休暇 (overtime; paid leave; regular employee; agency worker).

### 11.2 Business Email and Written Japanese

7. Put these parts of a business email in order: (a) よろしくお願いいたします, (b) 〇〇株式会社　田中様, (c) 件名, (d) お世話になっております, (e) the request, (f) 株式会社〇〇の山田でございます.
8. Which opener do you use to write to a contact you have not spoken to for a year?
9. Fill in the blank: 資料を（　　）いたしましたので、ご確認ください。("I have attached the materials, so please take a look.")
10. Translate into Japanese: "I would be grateful for your reply by Friday."
11. What job does つきましては do in an email, and what usually comes right after it?
12. Write the three-line opening of an email to Ms. Tanaka at 〇〇株式会社 from Yamada at 株式会社△△, whom she already works with.

### 11.3 Meetings, Phone Calls and Office Culture

13. Translate into English: 早速ですが、本題に入らせていただきます。
14. Your manager asks you to send the minutes by tomorrow. Which reply is safer, 了解しました or 承知いたしました, and why?
15. At the end of your pitch, the client says 「それはちょっと難しいですね」. What are they most likely telling you?
16. An outside caller asks for 鈴木部長, who has stepped away from their desk. Reply in Japanese, apologizing and saying Suzuki is away.
17. Translate into Japanese: "I will call you back when they return."
18. Name the three parts of 報連相 and give a one-line example of each from an office job.
19. You want your team to adopt a new project-management tool. Describe how you would use 根回し before the team meeting.

## Further Reading

- [Job Hunting Guide for International Students (JASSO)](https://www.jasso.go.jp/en/ryugaku/after_study_j/job/guide.html) — the Japan Student Services Organization's free annual guide to Japanese hiring, interviews and business etiquette, in English and Japanese.
- [BJT Business Japanese Proficiency Test](https://www.kanken.or.jp/bjt/english/) — the official site of the computer-based test that measures workplace Japanese, with sample questions that show what real business communication demands.
- [Jisho](https://jisho.org/) — a free Japanese-English dictionary that gives readings and example sentences for business vocabulary such as 見積もり or 手配.
- [*The Culture Map* by Erin Meyer](https://erinmeyer.com/books/the-culture-map/) (PublicAffairs, 2014) — a widely read book on how cultures differ in feedback, persuasion and decision-making, with Japan as a recurring example of high-context communication.

Business Japanese is one practical goal; for many learners the other is a certificate that proves their level to employers, schools and immigration. [Chapter 12](../12-the-jlpt/README.md) turns to the Japanese-Language Proficiency Test: how it works, what each level demands and how to prepare for it.
