# Chapter 10 — Japanese for Media

## 10.1 Japanese Through Anime

1. まさか！ — "No way!" / "Don't tell me…!"; そんな…！ — "It can't be…!"; 俺に任せろ！ — "Leave it to me!"; やれやれ — "Good grief."
2. 仲間 (*nakama*) — comrades, friends on your side; 異世界 (*isekai*) — another world; 必殺技 (*hissatsuwaza*) — signature finishing move; 部活 (*bukatsu*) — school club activities; 告白 (*kokuhaku*) — confession of love.
3. (a) The wise old mentor (わし, 〜のう). (b) A maid (お帰りなさいませ, ご主人様). (c) A tsundere (the stammered 「べ、別に」 denial). (d) An ojōsama, a rich girl (わたくし, 〜ませんの, よろしくってよ).
4. Model answers: 信じろ！ → 信じて！; 俺に任せろ！ → 任せて！ (or 私に任せて！); 行くぞ！ → 行こう！
5. Outraged disbelief: "You're telling me it failed!?" The 〜だと！？ pattern echoes what he has just heard and challenges it.
6. 俺 is a rough masculine "I," お前 is a blunt "you" that can sound rude or condescending, and 〜ぞ is a rough emphatic ending; all three are risky at work. 任せて ("leave it to me") is a friendly casual form, fine with a coworker you are close to, though 任せてください is safer.

## 10.2 Reading Manga

7. Top right, top left, bottom right, bottom left.
8. ドカン — an explosion; ザーザー — pouring rain; ガシャン — glass breaking; ポタポタ — dripping.
9. ドキドキ — a pounding heart; モジモジ — fidgeting shyly; ニヤリ — a sly grin; ガーン — shock.
10. "The classroom fell silent." / "The classroom went dead quiet."
11. 食べている; 忘れてしまった; 行かなければ (ならない); 知らないのだ.
12. The kanji 本気 gives the meaning, "seriously, for real," while the furigana マジ gives the voice: the character actually says the slangy マジ.
13. "I still haven't done my homework. I have to do it."

## 10.3 Reading the News

14. 米 — the United States; 中 — China; 韓 — South Korea; 露 — Russia; 英 — the United Kingdom; 仏 — France; 独 — Germany; 豪 — Australia.
15. 首相 (*shushō*) — prime minister; 与党 (*yotō*) — ruling party; 野党 (*yatō*) — opposition party; 選挙 (*senkyo*) — election; 地震 (*jishin*) — earthquake; 避難指示 (*hinan shiji*) — evacuation order.
16. 令和8年 = 2026; 平成7年 = 1995. 2026年度 runs from April 2026 to March 2027.
17. 1億2000万人 (*ichioku nisenman nin*) — 120 million people; 約3000人 (*yaku sanzen nin*) — about 3,000 people; 100人余り (*hyaku nin amari*) — just over 100 people; 3割強 (*sanwari kyō*) — a little over 30 percent.
18. Model answer: 日本とアメリカの首脳会談が来月開催される方向だ。 "A Japan–US summit is set to be held next month."
19. Model answer: 円安を受けて、物価の上昇が続いている。 "Prices continue to rise in the wake of the weak yen."
20. After the arrest he is 山田容疑者 (Yamada *yōgisha*, suspect Yamada); after indictment he is 山田被告 (Yamada *hikoku*, defendant Yamada). Since 1989 the media have avoided the bare name (呼び捨て) partly so as not to treat an unconvicted person as guilty.
21. 注意報 (level 2), 警報 (level 3), 危険警報 (level 4), 特別警報 (level 5).
