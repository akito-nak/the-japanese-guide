# Chapter 8 — Getting Around Japan

## 8.1 Trains and Transit

1. 改札 (*kaisatsu*): ticket gate. 乗り換え (*norikae*): transfer. 終電 (*shūden*): last train of the night. 各駅停車 (*kakueki teisha*): local train that stops at every station. 指定席 (*shiteiseki*): reserved seat.
2. 各駅停車 stops at every station. 特急 (limited express) is the one most likely to need an extra limited-express ticket on top of the basic fare.
3. A rapid train (快速, which skips some stations) bound for Shinjuku will depart from track 3.
4. "The next station is Shibuya, Shibuya. The exit (the doors) will be on the left side."
5. The fare adjustment machine, 乗り越し精算機 (*norikoshi seisanki*), next to the exit gates. Top up your card there and tap out again.
6. 京都駅までお願いします。(*Kyōto-eki made onegai shimasu.*) Then: 領収書をください。(*Ryōshūsho o kudasai.*)
7. この電車は品川に止まりますか。(*Kono densha wa Shinagawa ni tomarimasu ka.*) 乗り換えは何番線ですか。(*Norikae wa nanbansen desu ka.*)

## 8.2 Cities and Regions

8. 博多ラーメン: Fukuoka. 味噌カツ: Nagoya. ジンギスカン: Sapporo. ゴーヤーチャンプルー: Naha (Okinawa). たこ焼き: Osaka.
9. めっちゃええやん → とてもいいじゃない (or すごくいいね). ほんまにおおきに → 本当にありがとう. それはあかん → それはだめ.
10. 都道府県 lists the four kinds of prefecture: one 都 (Tokyo), one 道 (Hokkaido), two 府 (Osaka and Kyoto) and 43 県, for 47 in total.
11. She is from the Chūgoku region of western Honshū (Hiroshima, Okayama, Tottori, Shimane and Yamaguchi). 中国 on its own also means China, so a learner might think she is Chinese; 地方 (*chihō*, region) makes the meaning clear.
12. It means "no first-time customers": the teahouse accepts new guests only through an introduction from an existing customer. The custom protects long-standing relationships and lets the house vouch for every guest, and it is old merchant practice rather than a rule aimed at foreigners.

## 8.3 Shopping and Money

13. ¥3,980: 三千九百八十円 (*sanzen kyūhyaku hachijū-en*). ¥12,000: 一万二千円 (*ichiman nisen-en*). ¥30,000: 三万円 (*sanman-en*). ¥158,000: 十五万八千円 (*jūgoman hassen-en*).
14. 税込 (*zeikomi*) is the price including tax, 1,100 yen; 税抜 (*zeinuki*) is the price before the 10% consumption tax, 1,000 yen. You pay the tax-inclusive 1,100 yen.
15. 半額 (*hangaku*) means half price. Supermarkets mark down fresh food and bento near closing time.
16. Model answer: いいえ、持っていません。(*Iie, motte imasen.*) Then: 袋は大丈夫です。(*Fukuro wa daijōbu desu.*) or 袋はいりません。
17. カードで払えますか。(or カードは使えますか。) 見ているだけです。(casually, 見てるだけです。)
18. No, at neither store. Tax-free shopping needs at least ¥5,000 before tax at one store on one day, and purchases at different stores can't be combined.
19. From November 1, 2026, you pay the full price including the 10% tax at the store. Before checking in your luggage at the departure airport, and within 90 days of purchase, you show your passport and the camera at the customs tax-free kiosk; once customs confirms you are taking it out of Japan, the tax is refunded.

## 8.4 Emergencies

20. A stolen bag: 110 (police). A friend who has collapsed: 119 (ambulance). A kitchen fire: 119 (fire). In many prefectures, #7119 gives advice when you are unsure whether you need an ambulance.
21. 救急です。(*Kyūkyū desu.*) Then: 意識がありません。(*Ishiki ga arimasen.*) Be ready to give the address next.
22. 卵にアレルギーがあります。(*Tamago ni arerugī ga arimasu.*) パスポートをなくしました。(*Pasupōto o nakushimashita.*) ここが痛いです。(*Koko ga itai desu.*)
23. 震度5強 (*shindo go kyō*, "upper 5") is the strength of shaking at your location on the Japan Meteorological Agency's 0–7 scale: strong enough to topple furniture and make walking difficult. Magnitude 6.8 measures the energy released at the earthquake's source; it is the same everywhere, while shindo differs from town to town.
24. 避難指示 is an evacuation order at alert level 4: everyone in the designated area should evacuate now, to an evacuation area or shelter (or, if going outside is more dangerous, to a safer place such as an upper floor). Follow the instructions of local officials.
25. 津波警報 (*tsunami keihō*) is a tsunami warning. Leave the beach at once and move to high ground or the upper floors of a tall, solid building; don't wait to see the water, and don't return until the warning is lifted.
