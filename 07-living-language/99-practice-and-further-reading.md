# Practice and Further Reading

Slang is best learned by decoding it, and history by putting it in order, so this practice asks you to do both. For the slang items, also decide who you could say each word to; for the history items, try to give a date or century before you look anything up. Answers are at the back of the book. Remember that the slang answers describe usage as of 2026.

## Practice

### 7.1 Internet Language and Slang

1. What do these mean in a chat message? (a) www (b) 草 (c) 乙 (d) ちな
2. Explain why 草 came to mean laughter.
3. Give the meaning and origin of each word: (a) リア充 (b) ぼっち (c) バズる (d) 推し
4. Your friend complains: 既読スルーされた… What happened?
5. Decode these number puns: (a) 4649 (b) 0840 (c) 39
6. What feeling does each kaomoji show? (a) (^_^;) (b) (T_T) (c) orz
7. A friend writes この新曲、エモい. Translate it, and say whether エモい is positive or negative here.

### 7.2 Contemporary and Emerging Japanese

8. Translate into natural English: (a) それな。 (b) このラーメン、過去一おいしい。 (c) 推しが尊すぎてしんどい。
9. Explain the pattern 〜界隈 and translate 風呂キャンセル界隈.
10. A Japanese friend in her twenties tells her grandfather that his cooking is 普通においしい. He looks hurt. Why?
11. Turn these into verbs with 〜る and give the past tense of each: (a) Google (b) "buzz" (go viral) (c) "diss"
12. Your coworker is promoted to a big project and you want to say the role is too small for her talents. Why is 役不足 a risky word to use, and what could you say instead?
13. Which of these would sound dated or cringeworthy if used in earnest as of 2026? (a) それな (b) ぴえん超えてぱおん (c) インスタ映え (d) 過去一 (e) り
14. What is the 新語・流行語大賞, when is it announced, and why is it not a reliable guide to what people actually say?

### 7.3 How Japanese Evolved

15. Put these in chronological order: (a) the 2010 jōyō kanji revision (b) the Kojiki (c) the invention of emoji for i-mode (d) Futabatei Shimei's 浮雲 (e) The Tale of Genji (f) the 1946 kana spelling reform
16. What is man'yōgana, and how did it lead to hiragana and katakana?
17. 行 is read ぎょう in 行列, こう in 銀行 and あん in 行灯. Explain why one kanji has three on-readings.
18. Give the meaning of each Meiji-era coinage: (a) 哲学 (b) 社会 (c) 電話 (d) 自由
19. Match each loanword to the language it came from: パン, コーヒー, アルバイト, テレビ. Languages: Dutch, English, German, Portuguese.
20. Why is the topic particle は written with the kana for "ha" even though it is pronounced "wa"?
21. What was genbun itchi, and why did it matter for modern written Japanese?

## Further Reading

- [新語・流行語大賞 (New Words and Buzzwords Award)](https://www.jiyu.co.jp/singo/) — the official site of the yearly award, with every year's nominees and winners since 1984; read the top ten each December.
- [国語に関する世論調査 (Agency for Cultural Affairs)](https://www.bunka.go.jp/tokei_hakusho_shuppan/tokeichosa/kokugo_yoronchosa/) — the government's annual survey of how Japanese people actually use and understand words such as 役不足 and 確信犯; the primary source for meaning shifts.
- [常用漢字表 (Agency for Cultural Affairs)](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/kanji/) — the official 2010 list of 2,136 jōyō kanji, the end point of the postwar script reforms.
- [National Institute for Japanese Language and Linguistics (NINJAL)](https://www.ninjal.ac.jp/) — Japan's national research institute for the language, with corpora and publications on its history and change.
- [Tae Kim's Guide to Learning Japanese: Casual Patterns and Slang](https://guidetojapanese.org/learn/grammar/casual) — a free explanation of the casual grammar that most slang is built on.
- [Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/) — a large free Japanese online dictionary that includes many current slang and internet terms not yet in print dictionaries.
