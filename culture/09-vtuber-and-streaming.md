# Vtubers, Streaming & Live-Comment Culture — 配信文化 📺

> *"For a huge slice of young Japan, the dominant media isn't TV or even anime — it's livestreams. And live Japanese has its own vocabulary, written in scrolling comments at the speed of a slot machine. Learn it, and a whole world of natural, current Japanese opens up."*

Japanese streaming culture — from the pioneering **ニコニコ動画 (Niconico)** to today's **Vtuber** empires (ホロライブ, にじさんじ) — produced a rich, fast-moving vocabulary. It's some of the most *current* Japanese you can study, and unlike scripted anime, it's real people talking in real time. This page covers the comment-culture, the fan jargon, and the general streaming terms you'll meet everywhere.

> **Register key:** 【コメント】 = typed by viewers in live chat · 【ファン語】 = fan jargon you'll hear and read · 【死語気味】 = nostalgic/dated but still recognizable.

---

## Niconico & 弾幕 — Where Comment Culture Began

**ニコニコ動画** pioneered comments that **scroll across the video itself**, synced to the timestamp. When a big moment hits, the screen floods — that's **弾幕 (danmaku)**, "barrage," borrowed from bullet-hell shooters. This culture seeded almost all Japanese live-comment language, and much of it migrated to YouTube Live and Twitch.

| Term | Reading | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 888888 | (pachi-pachi) | **Applause.** 「8」reads *pa(chi)* — the clap sound — repeated | 【コメント】Very much alive; jumped to YouTube/Twitch JP |
| 草 / wwww | kusa | "lol" — 笑 → w → a field of w's looks like grass (草) | 【コメント】Mainstream everywhere now. 大草原 = harder lol |
| 主 | nushi | The uploader/streamer (from 投稿主). 生主 = live streamer | 【コメント】Current |
| 神回 | kami-kai | "God episode" — an exceptional stream or installment | 【コメント】Very alive, all platforms |
| うぽつ | upotsu | "Thanks for uploading" (アップ + 乙) | 【コメント】Still used, esp. on archives |
| おつ / 乙 | otsu | "Good work / thanks / cya" (from お疲れ様) | 【コメント】Fully mainstream |
| 公式が病気 | kōshiki ga byōki | "The official account is *sick*" — admiring disbelief at something gloriously over-the-top | 【ファン語】Niconico-born meme, still used |
| ｷﾀ━━━(ﾟ∀ﾟ)━━━!! | kitā | "It's here! / Here we go!" | 【死語気味】Recognizable but era-marked |
| 弾幕薄いぞ！ | danmaku usui zo | "The barrage is thin — what are you doing!" (demands more comments) | 【死語気味】Iconic *Gundam*-derived meme line |
| テラ〜 / ギガ〜 | tera / giga | SI-prefix intensifiers: ギガかわいい = "mega-cute" | 【死語気味】Nostalgic; ギガ survives faintly |

> **A note on ASCII art (AA):** Niconico and the textboard **2channel** developed elaborate multi-line Shift-JIS art and characters like **モナー (Monā)**, a cat whose catchphrase 「オマエモナー」("you too!") became a standard retort. It's nostalgic now, but it's the ancestor of today's emoji-and-sticker culture. See the [Internet & Slang](../foundations/08-internet-and-slang.md) page for more 2ch heritage.

## Vtuber Fan Vocabulary — The Current Core

The **Vtuber** (バーチャルYouTuber) boom — anonymous performers streaming as animated avatars — created the most active fan vocabulary in Japan today. This is current, high-frequency, and increasingly mainstream.

### The performer and the avatar

| Term | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 中の人 | naka no hito | "The person inside" — the performer behind the avatar (mildly sensitive to mention) |
| 魂 | tamashii | "Soul" — the performer, especially the voice. Same idea as 中の人 |
| ガワ | gawa | "Shell / skin" — the avatar model itself (as opposed to the 魂) |
| 前世 | zensei | "Past life" — the performer's prior identity/account. Often taboo to bring up on stream |
| 卒業 | sotsugyō | "Graduation" — the euphemism for retiring or leaving an agency (cf. 引退, a harder retirement) |

### Money, clips, and metrics

| Term | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| スパチャ | supacha | **Super Chat** — a paid, highlighted comment (YouTube tipping) |
| 赤スパ / スパチャ赤 | aka-supa | A "red superchat" — the top ¥10,000 tier, red banner |
| 投げ銭 | nagesen | "Tossed money" — tipping/gifting in general |
| 切り抜き | kirinuki | A **clip** — an edited excerpt of a stream |
| 切り抜き師 | kirinuki-shi | A "clipper" — someone who makes clips (often officially permitted) |
| 同接 | dōsetsu | **Concurrent viewers** (同時接続者数) — the key popularity metric |
| 案件 | anken | A sponsored / tie-in stream (paid promotion) |

### Fans and how they organize

| Term | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 推し | oshi | The person/character you support and stan |
| 推し活 | oshi-katsu | "Stanning activities" — everything you do to support your 推し |
| 単推し / 箱推し | tan-oshi / hako-oshi | Supporting one member only / supporting the whole "box" (agency) |
| ガチ恋 | gachi-koi | "Genuine love" — a fan really in romantic love with the talent (carries an over-invested edge) |
| ○○民 | -min | Fanbase suffix: "the ○○ folk" |
| リスナー | risunā | The viewers/listeners |
| 凸待ち / 凸 | totsu-machi / totsu | "Charge in" — waiting for others to call in / a collab call-in |
| 個人勢 / 企業勢 | kojin-zei / kigyō-zei | Independent Vtuber / agency-affiliated Vtuber |

> **Tone warning:** 信者 (shinja, "believer"), 養分 (yōbun, "nutrients" — a fan who keeps spending), アンチ (anti — hater), and ガチ恋 all carry a loaded or critical edge. They're not neutral — handle with care.

## General Streaming Japanese

Useful on YouTube, Twitch, and TikTok Live alike — the everyday plumbing of stream culture.

| Term | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 配信 / 生配信 | haishin / nama-haishin | Stream / live stream |
| 配信者 | haishin-sha | Streamer (cf. ストリーマー, 実況者 for game commentary) |
| アーカイブ | ākaibu | The VOD — the recording left up afterward. アーカイブ勢 = watch-later viewers |
| チャンネル登録 | channeru tōroku | "Subscribe" |
| 高評価 | kō-hyōka | "Like" (thumbs-up). 「高評価よろしく」= "please like" |
| 待機 | taiki | "Standing by" before a stream starts. Viewers type 待機 in chat; 待機画面 = the waiting screen |
| 初見 | shoken | A first-time viewer. 「初見です」in chat; 初見さん歓迎 = newcomers welcome |
| 定期 | teiki | "Recurring" — tag for a habitual comment or a scheduled stream |
| モデレーター / モデ | moderētā / mode | Moderator / "mod" |
| 荒らし | arashi | Troll / chat griefer |

## SNS Platform Language (TikTok / Instagram / X)

The places slang is *born* before it reaches anywhere else.

| Term | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| おすすめ | osusume | The "For You" / recommended feed. 「おすすめに乗る」= hit the FYP |
| バズり / バズる | bazuri / bazuru | To go viral (from "buzz") |
| 拡散 | kakusan | To spread/signal-boost. 「拡散希望」= please share |
| 映え | bae | Photogenic (from インスタ映え — the root survives; the full phrase is dated) |
| ストーリー / リール | sutōrī / rīru | Instagram Stories / Reels |
| 裏垢 / 鍵垢 / 病み垢 | ura-aka / kagi-aka / yami-aka | Alt account / locked (private) account / "venting" mental-health account (垢 = アカウント) |
| リプ | ripu | A reply (cf. ふぁぼ "fav," now dated — replaced by いいね) |
| 空リプ | kara-ripu | "Empty reply" — posting *at* someone without @-mentioning them |
| 引用 | in'yō | Quote-post (quote tweet). Post-rebrand: ツイート→ポスト, リツイート→リポスト |
| FF外から失礼します | FF-gai kara shitsurei shimasu | "Excuse me, replying as a non-mutual" (FF = Follow/Follower) — polite preface to a stranger; reads slightly formal/older now |

## How to Use This for Learning

Streaming is a goldmine *because* it's unscripted, but pace yourself:

1. **Start with 切り抜き (clips).** Bite-sized, often subtitled by fans, and they pick the funniest/clearest moments.
2. **Read the live chat.** It's a real-time vocabulary drill — you'll see 草, 888888, 初見です, and 待機 used in context within minutes.
3. **Pick a streamer who speaks clearly.** Variety/chatting (雑談配信) streams are better for language than fast-paced gameplay.
4. **Cross-reference with the [Contemporary Japanese page](../foundations/09-contemporary-japanese.md)** — streamers are where this year's slang shows up first.

> **Reality check:** Some of this vocabulary (the niconico-era memes especially) is nostalgic, and fan jargon assumes you're inside the culture. Don't lead with スパチャ slang in a job interview. But for connecting with young Japanese people and consuming the media they actually consume, this is the real, living language.
